Design a fed-batch medium, then check whether your run adds up

Five steps. You enter the organism, the recipe, the vessel and what you want to make; the engine works out which nutrient runs out first, how much oxygen the vessel has to supply, and — once you have run it — whether your measurements balance.

Shared

What the recipe delivers, per litre of everything you deliver. The one quantity both halves of the model use.

Design

What your recipe could support: limiting nutrient, expected titres, the kLa the vessel must deliver.

Measured

What your run actually did: carbon recovery, an independent oxygen check, achieved yields.

Steps 1 to 3 are enough for a design answer. Step 4 is where you add a product, and the measured-run fields, if you have already run the fermentation — leave them blank and you simply get the design side.
